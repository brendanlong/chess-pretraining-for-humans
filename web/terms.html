Move Trainer is a free research prototype run by Brendan Long. Using it means accepting these terms. They're short, and the short version is: it's an experiment, your answers are its data, and it comes with no promises.

What you're using

A chess move-discrimination trainer built to test whether dense labeled practice sharpens chess perception. It is not a product, not a coaching service, and not a guarantee that your play will improve. The source is public on GitHub under the MIT license.

Using it fairly

  • You should be at least 13 years old.
  • An account is for one person. Don't share it, and don't try to reach anyone else's.
  • Don't attack the service: no automated hammering, no scraping the item bank in bulk, no attempts to break authentication or degrade it for other people.
  • You're responsible for your password. If you didn't give an email address, there is no reset path — a lost password means a lost account.

Accounts that abuse the service may be suspended or deleted.

The privacy policy spells out exactly what's in that data — never your username or email.
