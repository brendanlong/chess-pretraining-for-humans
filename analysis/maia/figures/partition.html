How to read it. Segments run from Maia agreeing with Stockfish (left) to Maia playing something worse than the blunder we already use as the wrong answer (right). The divider marks the only line that matters if Maia were used to source the answer: to its right, the “correct” move would be no better than the distractor.
Detail. Of the 23.5% that are as bad as the human's move, 20.3% of the bank is Maia playing that exact move. Of the 16.4% near‑best, only 1.5% of the bank is the case where Maia plays well but rates Stockfish's move implausible (P < 0.01).
Method. Maia‑2 rapid at rating 1500. The two leftmost shares are exact over the full bank; the rest are estimated from a 2,500‑item random sample of the 21,999 disagreements, scored by Stockfish at depth 18. “Within 0.03” is trainer.mine's own error floor, and is load‑bearing: search noise here has sd 0.017.
